# Intellectual Property Notes

## Potentially relevant areas

- Center-spine/composite-wing system architecture
- Wing attachment and tolerance strategy
- Integrated graphic skin and composite binding treatment
- Internal structural geometry only if technically novel and supported
- Modular manufacturing or service method

## Controls

- Record dated concepts and decisions in Git.
- Preserve named human contributors and source references.
- Search prior art before asserting novelty.
- Avoid detailed public disclosure before deciding whether formal protection is warranted.
- Treat AI-generated suggestions as research leads, not proof of novelty or ownership.

This note is not legal advice. A qualified IP attorney should review protectable claims before public technical disclosure or investment outreach.
