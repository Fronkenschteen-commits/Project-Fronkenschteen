# Ethical Development Standard

## Non-negotiable principles

- No child labor, forced labor, exploitative labor, or knowingly unsafe working conditions.
- No wood, shell, polymer, coating, adhesive, or composite whose sourcing or use violates applicable law or the project's ethical standard.
- Avoid environmentally unsound materials and processes when a credible safer alternative exists.
- Do not make environmental, acoustic, performance, origin, or AI-collaboration claims that cannot be substantiated.
- Protect contributors' credit, consent, confidential information, and intellectual property.
- Human judgment remains responsible for consequential approvals.

## Decision screen

Before selecting a material, supplier, fabricator, or business partner, record:

1. Origin and traceability
2. Labor practices
3. Worker and user safety
4. Environmental burden
5. Legal/compliance status
6. Repairability and end-of-life options
7. Alternatives considered
8. Evidence and unresolved concerns

## Approval rule

Cost or schedule savings do not override a serious ethical concern. An unresolved critical concern blocks approval until TJ explicitly resolves it with documented evidence.
