# Project Charter

## Objective

Develop and validate a modular electric-guitar platform centered on a traditional tonewood structural spine with visually expressive composite wing modules.

## Initial scope

- Product requirements and design language
- Center-spine and composite-wing architecture
- Wing attachment and interface design
- Full-graphic and natural-wood visual variants
- Neck, fretboard, bridge, tuning, electronics, and service access
- CAD, structural and modal analysis, test planning, and prototypes
- Ethical sourcing and manufacturing criteria
- Cost, manufacturability, IP, and external player validation

## Out of scope for the first baseline

- Production launch
- Unvalidated performance or acoustic marketing claims
- Full automation of engineering-document approval
- Large-team collaboration infrastructure

## Success at prototype gate

- Stable tuning and professional setup
- Safe, structurally sound assembly
- Comfortable weight and ergonomics
- Repeatable wing-to-spine manufacture
- Credible A/B evidence for novel design elements
- Strong fit and finish
- Positive structured feedback from qualified players
- Traceable ethical sourcing and process choices
