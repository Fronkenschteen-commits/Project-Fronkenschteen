# Project Constitution

## Purpose

Project Fronkenschteen will develop a distinctive, musically credible, manufacturable electric-guitar platform through disciplined human-AI collaboration.

## Governing principles

1. **Musical usefulness first.** Beauty and novelty do not compensate for poor playability, tuning stability, reliability, or tone.
2. **Tuning stability is a critical quality signal.** A prototype must remain convincingly in tune during realistic evaluation by experienced players.
3. **Evidence over enthusiasm.** Established facts, hypotheses, assumptions, inferences, and research questions must be labeled distinctly.
4. **Use proven luthiery where appropriate.** Do not spend scarce validation effort re-proving mature neck, fret, adhesive, and setup practices without a project-specific reason.
5. **Innovate deliberately.** Focus original research on composite wings, attachment interfaces, internal structures, art integration, resonance behavior, serviceability, and manufacturing.
6. **Human ethical judgment governs.** No child labor, exploitative labor, environmentally irresponsible materials or processes, deceptive claims, or avoidable harm.
7. **Restraint is a design value.** Aim small, miss small; pursue elegance instead of decorative or technical excess.
8. **No acoustic mythology.** Claims about sustain, resonance, tonewoods, metamaterials, Fibonacci geometry, or waveguides require evidence appropriate to the claim.
9. **Design for manufacture and repair.** Cost, tolerances, repeatability, assembly, serviceability, and end-of-life impacts are engineering requirements.
10. **Preserve reasoning.** Significant decisions record rationale, alternatives, evidence, risks, and validation.

## Authority

TJ retains final project authority and approval responsibility. Miles structures evidence, maintains systems coherence, identifies unsupported claims, and recommends validation.
