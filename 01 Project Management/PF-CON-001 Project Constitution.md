# Project Constitution

## Purpose

Project Fronkenschteen will develop a distinctive, musically credible, manufacturable electric-guitar platform through disciplined human-AI collaboration.

## Governing principles

1. **Musical usefulness first.** Beauty and novelty do not compensate for poor playability, tuning stability, reliability, or tone.
2. **Tuning stability is a critical quality signal.** A prototype must remain convincingly in tune during realistic evaluation by experienced players.
3. **Evidence over enthusiasm.** Established facts, hypotheses, assumptions, inferences, and research questions must be labeled distinctly.
4. **Use proven luthiery where appropriate.** Do not spend scarce validation effort re-proving mature neck, fret, adhesive, fastener, hardware, and setup practices without a project-specific reason. Established practice may be revisited when project evidence identifies it as a limiting factor.
5. **Innovate deliberately.** Focus original research on composite wings, attachment and material interfaces, internal structures, art integration, resonance behavior, serviceability, and manufacturing.
6. **AI amplifies human agency.** Artificial intelligence shall be used to amplify human creativity, engineering discipline, and decision quality—not to replace human authorship, responsibility, or vision. TJ retains project authority and final decision responsibility; AI collaborators provide analysis, critique, organization, research support, and multidisciplinary perspective.
7. **Human ethical judgment governs.** No child labor, exploitative labor, environmentally irresponsible materials or processes, deceptive claims, or avoidable harm.
8. **Restraint is a design value.** Aim small, miss small; pursue elegance instead of decorative or technical excess.
9. **No acoustic mythology.** Claims about sustain, resonance, tonewoods, metamaterials, Fibonacci geometry, or waveguides require evidence appropriate to the claim.
10. **Design for manufacture and repair.** Cost, tolerances, repeatability, assembly, serviceability, and end-of-life impacts are engineering requirements.
11. **Preserve reasoning.** Significant decisions record rationale, alternatives, evidence, risks, and validation or verification when appropriate. Established conventional choices do not require new experiments merely for procedural completeness.
12. **Controlled records govern.** Conversation generates ideas and rationale; controlled repository documents preserve project knowledge; approved baselines define authoritative project state.

## Authority

TJ retains final project authority, human authorship accountability, and approval responsibility. Miles structures evidence, maintains systems coherence and traceability, identifies unsupported claims and risks, and recommends validation where it adds project value.
