# Risk Register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Response |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| PF-RSK-001 | Composite wings or joints introduce creep, looseness, or instability | Medium | High | Coupon tests; tolerance studies; environmental cycling |
| PF-RSK-002 | Novel internal geometry adds weight/cost without measurable benefit | High | Medium | Compare against simple control structures |
| PF-RSK-003 | Prototype fails professional tuning-stability expectations | Medium | Critical | Treat tuners, nut, bridge, neck stiffness, setup, and stringing as a system |
| PF-RSK-004 | Long wing interfaces create visible gaps or finishing defects | Medium | High | Datum strategy, tolerance stack, sample joints, finishing trials |
| PF-RSK-005 | Graphic skins, veneer, or coating delaminate or wear poorly | Medium | High | Adhesion and environmental tests |
| PF-RSK-006 | Unsupported acoustic language damages credibility | Medium | High | Evidence labels and claim review |
| PF-RSK-007 | Material or supplier conflicts with ethical principles | Medium | Critical | Supplier declarations, traceability, and exclusion criteria |
| PF-RSK-008 | Documentation overhead consumes limited design time | Medium | Medium | Keep records concise; review registers on cadence |
| PF-RSK-009 | Premature external disclosure weakens IP position | Medium | High | IP review before public technical disclosure |
