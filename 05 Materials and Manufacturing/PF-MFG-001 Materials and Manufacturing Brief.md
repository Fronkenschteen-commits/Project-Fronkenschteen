# Materials and Manufacturing Brief

## Current material directions

- Mahogany center spine and neck are leading directions.
- Rosewood or ebony fretboard remains to be selected.
- Composite-wing material is unresolved; carbon-fiber nylon and other printed polymers are candidates, not commitments.
- Graphic composite skin, veneer, faux binding, and coatings require compatibility testing.

## Manufacturing questions

- What processes and datums control the long wing-to-spine interfaces?
- Can acceptable fit survive printing, machining, finishing, and environmental variation?
- Should wing attachment be adhesive, press-fit, mechanical, or hybrid?
- How will electronics be installed, shielded, serviced, and replaced?
- Can aesthetic seams remain intentional across production variation?
- Which materials are recyclable, repairable, traceable, and safe to fabricate?

## Early test articles

1. Material and finish coupons
2. Straight dovetail/interface coupons at several clearances
3. Curved or body-representative interface segment
4. Graphic skin and binding sample
5. Control-cavity cover and fastener sample
6. Instrument-scale mass and balance mockup
