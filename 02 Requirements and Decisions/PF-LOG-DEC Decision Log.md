# Decision Log

These entries capture prior direction. TJ will confirm status during baseline review.

| ID | Decision | Rationale | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| PF-D-0001 | Use a tonewood center-spine/composite-wing architecture as the primary concept | Stiff structural path, modularity, visual differentiation | Provisional |
| PF-D-0002 | Treat tuning stability as a critical first-impression and fit-and-finish requirement | Professional players quickly notice instability | Provisional |
| PF-D-0003 | Use conventional luthiery norms where practice is well established | Conserve validation effort for genuine innovation | Provisional |
| PF-D-0004 | Develop full-graphic and natural-wood visual variants | Support expressive art and restrained craftsmanship | Provisional |
| PF-D-0005 | Prefer glue and engineered joint fit over unnecessary metal fasteners at wing interfaces | Aesthetic continuity and potential assembly benefits | Provisional; test required |
| PF-D-0006 | Treat visible joinery primarily as an aesthetic opportunity until engineering evidence supports performance claims | Avoid assuming acoustic benefit | Provisional |
| PF-D-0007 | Use high-resolution printable composite surfaces and visible composite binding as design directions | Graphic continuity and platform identity | Provisional |
| PF-D-0008 | Human ethical judgment governs sourcing, manufacture, and business decisions | Core project value | Proposed Approved |
| PF-D-0009 | Keep Fibonacci-inspired geometry as a testable hypothesis, not a design truth | Acoustic benefit is unproven | Provisional |
| PF-D-0010 | Delay outreach to influential players until a prototype reaches a genuine “wow” stage | Protect credibility and maximize feedback value | Provisional |
