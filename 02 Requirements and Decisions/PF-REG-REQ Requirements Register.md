# Requirements Register

These are proposed requirements pending TJ's baseline review.

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Verification | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PF-REQ-001 | The instrument shall maintain professional-grade tuning stability during a defined playing test | Critical | Instrument test | Proposed |
| PF-REQ-002 | The platform shall use a tonewood center spine as its principal structural element | Critical | Design review | Proposed |
| PF-REQ-003 | Composite wing modules shall attach repeatably without unacceptable gaps, movement, or finish damage | Critical | Joint and prototype tests | Proposed |
| PF-REQ-004 | The design shall use accepted luthiery practice for mature subsystems unless a deviation has a documented benefit | High | Design review | Proposed |
| PF-REQ-005 | Novel acoustic or structural claims shall be supported by analysis or controlled physical testing | Critical | Evidence review | Proposed |
| PF-REQ-006 | The instrument shall be serviceable using practical tools and documented procedures | High | Service trial | Proposed |
| PF-REQ-007 | The design shall support a full-graphic visual treatment | High | Prototype inspection | Proposed |
| PF-REQ-008 | The design shall support a restrained natural-wood treatment | High | Prototype inspection | Proposed |
| PF-REQ-009 | Materials and processes shall meet the Ethical Development Standard | Critical | Supplier/process audit | Proposed |
| PF-REQ-010 | Weight and balance shall be acceptable during standing and seated play | High | Player evaluation | Proposed |
| PF-REQ-011 | Electrical controls shall be reliable, shielded, serviceable, and musically useful | High | Electrical and player tests | Proposed |
| PF-REQ-012 | The first detailed layout shall evaluate a 25.5-inch scale and 22 frets | Medium | Architecture review | Proposed |
| PF-REQ-013 | Mother-of-pearl position markers shall be evaluated in preference to conventional dots | Low | Industrial-design review | Proposed |
