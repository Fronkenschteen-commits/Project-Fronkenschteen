# Design Brief

## Product character

A refined electric guitar that combines a familiar, professionally credible playing experience with a visibly new composite-and-wood architecture.

## Architecture

- Mahogany is the leading center-spine and neck material direction; alternatives require explicit trade study.
- Composite upper and lower wing modules interface with the center spine.
- A set-neck or other accepted high-quality neck construction remains under review; do not let visible joinery override reliability.
- A 25.5-inch, 22-fret layout is the current evaluation target, not yet an approved production requirement.

## Visual variants

### Variant A — Full Graphic Top

- High-resolution graphic surface spanning the body presentation area
- Composite perimeter/binding visible as a deliberate frame
- Clean hardware and control integration

### Variant B — Natural Wood Focus

- Mahogany or other approved center wood visually emphasized
- Composite joinery and edge transitions used with restraint
- Material honesty and craftsmanship prioritized

## Shared aesthetic principles

- Elegance and restraint
- Strong proportions and intentional transitions
- No ornamental feature without a visual or functional purpose
- Japanese influence may inform proportion, restraint, and joinery expression without becoming pastiche
- Mother-of-pearl inlays preferred for evaluation over ordinary dots

## Functional priorities

1. Tuning stability
2. Playability and setup
3. Structural reliability
4. Weight and balance
5. Fit and finish
6. Musically useful electronics
7. Measurable performance of novel structures
